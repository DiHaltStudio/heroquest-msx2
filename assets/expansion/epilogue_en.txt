# Epilogue shown when the Witch Lord dies, two pages.
== 1
EPILOGUE

THE WITCH LORD IS DEFEATED AND HIS ARMY OF THE DEAD STILL SLEEPS BENEATH THE EARTH.

HIS SERVANTS WHO ESCAPED REMAIN ACTIVE. THEY WILL SURELY SEEK REVENGE ON THOSE WHO DEFEATED THEIR MASTER.

SKULMAR, WHOM WE THOUGHT DEAD, HAS FLED THE RUINS OF KALOS WITH MANY OF THE MOST POWERFUL SPELL BOOKS OF THE WITCH LORD. THE TRAITOR SIR RAGNAR HELPED HIM, THE MAN YOU ONCE RESCUED FROM THE FORCES OF CHAOS.

== 2
I FEAR IT WILL NOT BE LONG BEFORE YOU MUST FACE SKULMAR AND TRY TO END THIS THREAT FOREVER, ALTHOUGH SIR RAGNAR HAS BEEN CAPTURED AND EXECUTED.

BUT FOR NOW YOU HAVE EARNED A REST. COME, MY FRIENDS, THE EMPEROR AWAITS YOU IN THE GREAT PALACE. TONIGHT YOUR INCREDIBLE FEATS OF HEROISM WILL BE HONOURED BEFORE THE NOBILITY OF THE ENTIRE EMPIRE.
