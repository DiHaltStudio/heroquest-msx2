# Return of the Witch Lord. Quest numbers continue from the base game.

== 14: THE GATE OF DOOM
THE FORTRESS ENTRANCE IS EASY TO FIND, FOR NO ONE HAS TRIED TO HIDE IT. THE OUTER DOOR IS OPEN AND MANY CREATURES HAVE PASSED THROUGH IN RECENT DAYS.
"MAKE YOUR WAY THROUGH THE DREADED TUNNELS TO THE GATE OF DOOM. YOUR DESTINATION LIES BEYOND.
TREAD CAREFULLY, MY FRIENDS. THE ENEMY HAS HAD MANY DAYS TO PREPARE YOUR WELCOME."

== 15: THE COLD HALLS
THESE ICY PASSAGES ARE THE RESTING PLACE OF THE FEARED SPIRIT RIDERS WHO ONCE LED THE SKELETON CAVALRY OF THE WITCH LORD INTO BATTLE.
NOW THEY DREAM OF PAST GLORIES, WAITING TO BE CALLED AGAIN BENEATH THE BLACK BANNER.

== 16: THE SILENT PASSAGES
THESE PASSAGES HAVE BEEN SILENT FOR MORE THAN A THOUSAND YEARS.
THE LAST VOICES HEARD IN THESE SACRED HALLS WERE THOSE OF KING AGRAIN AND HIS PEOPLE, RUSHING TO DEFEND THE CITY.
ANCIENT AND TERRIBLE MAGIC IS SAID TO WORK HERE.
TREAD WITH CAUTION, MY FRIENDS.

== 17: THE HALLS OF VISION
KING AGRAIN WAS ONE OF THE GREAT KINGS WHO RULED BEFORE CHAOS.
HE WAS A FEARED WARRIOR AND HIS LAWS WERE KNOWN FOR THEIR JUSTICE.
AGRAIN ALSO DABBLED IN MAGIC. HE CREATED THE HALLS OF VISION, A MAZE OF MAGICAL ROOMS WHOSE SECRET LIES IN THE KEYS OF AGRAIN.

== 18: THE GATE OF BELLTHOR
HERE BEGINS THE TRUE DOMAIN OF THE WITCH LORD. IN THESE DEEP CAVERNS HE HAS BUILT HIS UNDERGROUND FORTRESS, FROM WHICH HE PLOTS TO DESTROY THE EMPIRE.
"LORETOME HAS TOLD ME OF A TERRIBLE GUARDIAN, BELLTHOR, WHO WATCHES THE GATE. YOU MUST FACE BELLTHOR TOGETHER IF YOU ARE TO WIN."

== 19: THE HALLS OF THE DEAD
YOU ARE ENTERING THE HALLS OF THE DEAD, WHERE THE WITCH LORD GATHERS HIS FOUL ARMY.
HIS PRESENCE CAN BE FELT NOW. STRIKE BEFORE HE ESCAPES.
GOOD LUCK.
YOUR SKILL AND CUNNING ARE YOUR ONLY HOPE.

== 20: THE FORGOTTEN LEGION
YOU HAVE ESCAPED THE WITCH LORD, BUT HE WILL SOON FOLLOW.
THE HALLS AHEAD HOUSE THE FORGOTTEN LEGION, THE ELITE OF HIS ARMY. SKULMAR COMMANDS THEM.
THEY ARE FEARSOME IN BATTLE, BEARING THE SCARS OF A THOUSAND FIGHTS, EACH A VICTORY FOR CHAOS.

== 21: THE FORBIDDEN CITY
NO MAN HAS EVER ENTERED THE FORBIDDEN CITY. EVEN LORETOME KNOWS LITTLE OF WHAT AWAITS YOU. ITS PASSAGES ARE SAID TO BE FULL OF UNDEAD, THE MINDLESS SERVANTS OF MORCAR, DOOMED TO WANDER THERE FOREVER.
BEWARE ALSO OF THE CITY ITSELF. IT IS CRUMBLING AND COULD COLLAPSE AT ANY MOMENT.

== 22: THE LAST GATE
THIS, MY FRIENDS, IS THE LAST GATE BEFORE THE COURT OF THE WITCH LORD.
THE PASSAGES BEYOND ARE GUARDED BY THE DOOMGUARD, WARRIORS OF CHAOS.
TAKE CARE, FOR THEY ARE MIGHTIER THAN THE CHAOS WARRIORS YOU HAVE FOUGHT BEFORE.

== 23: THE COURT OF THE WITCH LORD
THE WITCH LORD HAS WATCHED YOUR ADVANCE. HE HAS TRIED TO DESTROY YOU, BUT YOU HAVE THWARTED HIM EVERY TIME.
NOW HE HAS FLED TO HIS THRONE ROOM. THERE YOU MUST FACE HIM IN BATTLE. THIS TIME HE MUST NOT ESCAPE.
