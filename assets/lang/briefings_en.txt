# Base game briefings. Capital letters, digits, comma, full stop and quotes.
# Quest numbers are zero based; each nonempty line is a paragraph.

== 0: THE MAZE
THIS IS YOUR FINAL TEST BEFORE YOU SET OUT ON REAL ADVENTURES. USE ALL THE SKILLS YOU HAVE LEARNED.
FIND THE EXIT. SEVERAL MONSTERS ROAM THE MAZE AND WILL TRY TO STOP YOU, SO TAKE CARE.
THE FIRST TO ESCAPE RECEIVES 100 GOLD COINS TO BUY EQUIPMENT FOR FUTURE ADVENTURES.

== 1: THE RESCUE OF SIR RAGNAR
SIR RAGNAR, ONE OF THE MIGHTIEST KNIGHTS OF THE EMPEROR, HAS BEEN KIDNAPPED BY ULAG, THE ORC WARLORD.
FIND SIR RAGNAR AND BRING HIM BACK SAFELY. PRINCE MAGNUS WILL PAY 200 GOLD COINS FOR HIS RESCUE.
YOU MAY SHARE THE REWARD, BUT NOTHING WILL BE PAID IF SIR RAGNAR DIES DURING THE ESCAPE.

== 2: LAIR OF THE ORC WARLORD
PRINCE MAGNUS HAS ORDERED ULAG, THE ORC WARLORD WHO KIDNAPPED SIR RAGNAR, HUNTED DOWN AND KILLED.
WHOEVER KILLS ULAG RECEIVES 100 GOLD COINS AND MAY KEEP ANY TREASURE FOUND IN HIS STRONGHOLD.

== 3: PRINCE MAGNUS GOLD
THREE TREASURE CHESTS WERE STOLEN ON THEIR WAY TO THE EMPEROR. A REWARD OF 200 GOLD COINS IS OFFERED FOR THEIR RETURN WITH ALL THE GOLD.
THE THIEVES ARE AN ORC BAND HIDING IN THE BLACK MOUNTAINS. THEIR CAPTAIN IS GULTHOR.

== 4: MELARS MAZE
LONG AGO, THE MIGHTY WIZARD MELAR MADE A TALISMAN THAT INCREASED MAGICAL POWERS. HE KEPT IT CLOSE, FEARING IT MIGHT BE STOLEN AND USED BY THE ALLIES OF MORCAR.
IT IS SAID HE LEFT IT IN HIS LABORATORY AT THE HEART OF THE MAZE. THE PLACE IS FIERCELY GUARDED.
SOME SAY THE SPIRITS OF THOSE WHO DIED SEEKING THE TALISMAN STILL HAUNT THE MAZE.

== 5: LEGACY OF THE ORC WARLORD
GRAK, THE FOUL SON OF ULAG, HAS SWORN REVENGE ON THOSE WHO KILLED HIS FATHER. DESPITE YOUR HEAD START, HE TRACKED YOU DOWN AND CAPTURED YOU IN AN AMBUSH.
NOW YOU ARE HELD IN A DUNGEON WHILE HE PLANS A TERRIBLE PUNISHMENT.
AS THE GUARD SLEEPS, YOU PICK THE CELL LOCK WITH AN OLD RAT BONE. YOU MUST FIND YOUR EQUIPMENT AND ESCAPE.

== 6: THE STONE HUNTER
KARLEN, THE PERSONAL WIZARD OF THE EMPEROR, HAS VANISHED. THE EMPEROR FEARS HE HAS BEEN KILLED OR SEDUCED BY CHAOS MAGIC.
FIND OUT WHAT HAPPENED TO KARLEN AND BRING HIM BACK SAFELY IF HE IS ALIVE.
EACH OF YOU WILL RECEIVE 100 GOLD COINS FOR HIS RESCUE.

== 7: THE FIRE MAGE
ORCS FROM THE BLACK MOUNTAINS HAVE USED FIRE MAGIC IN THEIR RAIDS. BALUR, THE FIRE MAGE, IS RESPONSIBLE.
THE WIZARDS OF THE EMPEROR CANNOT COUNTER HIS SPELLS. YOU HAVE BEEN CHOSEN TO ENTER HIS LAIR BENEATH BLACK FIRE CRAG.
THE EMPEROR WILL PAY EACH OF YOU 150 GOLD COINS FOR THE DESTRUCTION OF BALUR.

== 8: RACE AGAINST TIME
A GUIDE HAS LED YOU INTO A DUNGEON SAID TO HOLD A GREAT SECRET. AFTER MANY DARK CORRIDORS YOU REACH A ROOM WITH THREE DOORS.
SUDDENLY HE PUTS OUT HIS TORCH. IN THE DARKNESS YOU HEAR HIM LAUGH AND SAY, "FAREWELL, HERO."
TO YOUR HORROR, IT IS A TRAP. YOU MUST ESCAPE OR PERISH IN THIS DARK, FORGOTTEN HOLE.

== 9: THE MYSTERIOUS CASTLE
LONG AGO THE WIZARD OLLAR FOUND THE ENTRANCE TO A GOLD MINE. USING HIS POWERS, HE BUILT AN ENCHANTED CASTLE OVER IT FOR PROTECTION.
THE CASTLE HAS MAGICAL DOORS AND IS GUARDED BY AN ARMY OF MONSTERS.
FIND THE MINE ENTRANCE IF YOU CAN. OTHERS HAVE TRIED, BUT THE CASTLE HAS ALWAYS FOILED THEM.

== 10: BASTION OF CHAOS
ORCS AND GOBLINS PLAGUE THE EASTERN LANDS. THE EMPEROR HAS ORDERED A BAND OF HEROES TO DESTROY THEM.
THE ORCS SHELTER IN AN UNDERGROUND STRONGHOLD, THE BASTION OF CHAOS, LED BY A BAND OF CHAOS WARRIORS.
FIGHT YOUR WAY THROUGH AND KILL EVERY MONSTER YOU FIND.
YOU WILL RECEIVE 10 GOLD COINS PER GOBLIN, 20 PER ORC, AND 30 PER FIMIR OR CHAOS WARRIOR KILLED.

== 11: BARAK TOR
WAR WITH THE EASTERN ORCS IS NEAR. THE EMPEROR MUST UNITE THE LESSER KINGDOMS. HE NEEDS THE STAR OF THE WEST, WORN BY LEGENDARY KINGS AND BY ROGAR WHEN HE FOUGHT MORCAR LONG AGO.
WHOEVER FINDS THE JEWEL RECEIVES 200 GOLD COINS. IT LIES IN BARAK TOR, THE RESTING PLACE OF THE WITCH LORD, THE KING OF THE DEAD.
THE SPIRIT BLADE IS THE ONLY WEAPON THAT CAN HARM HIM.

== 12: THE SPIRIT BLADE
THE WITCH LORD HAS AWAKENED. HE WILL BE A GRAVE THREAT TO THE EMPEROR IN THE COMING WAR. HE MUST BE DESTROYED BEFORE HE CAN RAISE HIS UNDEAD ARMY.
FIRST YOU MUST FIND THE SPIRIT BLADE, THE ONLY WEAPON THAT CAN DEFEAT HIM. IT WAS FORGED BY THE DWARFS OF THE WORLDS EDGE MOUNTAINS AND TEMPERED IN THE ELVEN FOUNTAINS OF LEBIN.
IT LIES IN AN ANCIENT RUINED TEMPLE. YOU MUST RECOVER IT.

== 13: RETURN TO BARAK TOR
NOW THAT YOU HAVE THE SPIRIT BLADE, RETURN TO BARAK TOR AND DEFEAT THE WITCH LORD.
THE EMPEROR RIDES TO MEET THE ORCS AT BLACK FIRE PASS.
IF YOU FAIL, THE WITCH LORD WILL RAISE HIS UNDEAD ARMY AND ATTACK THE IMPERIAL FORCES. THEN NOTHING WILL STOP CHAOS FROM INVADING THE LAND.
