This unofficial adaptation by DiHalt is a homage made for study and preservation. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the owners of the original game.

This version is based on the ZX Spectrum 128K program, using the PC version's sixteen-colour graphics, the Amiga version's introduction and music, and the adventures from \textit{Return of the Witch Lord}.

\textit{HeroQuest} is a trademark of its respective owners. The original computer game is \copyright{} 1991 Gremlin Graphics Software Limited, based on the board game created in collaboration with Games Workshop.

The cover illustration and game artwork belong to their respective owners and are included as part of this homage. Screenshots show this MSX2 version. The DiHalt logo identifies the adaptation's author.

MSX is a trademark of MSX Licensing Corporation. The MSX logo identifies the platform (MSX-Logo.svg, Wikimedia Commons).
