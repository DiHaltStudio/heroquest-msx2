\chapter{Morcar's adventures}
These are the original game's fourteen adventures, in the recommended order. Nothing needs loading from the cartridge: select them directly on the first page of the list.
\dospantallas{sala_q1}{sala_q6}{The beginning of two adventures.}
\aventura{1}{The Maze}
``This will be your final test before you set out on real adventures.'' Mentor has locked his pupils in a maze and released several monsters inside.
\objetivo{Find the exit. The first to reach it receives 100 gold coins to buy equipment.}
\consejos{The heroes start separately, each in a corner. This is the ideal adventure for learning to move, open doors and fight. There is no need to kill every monster, and the wizard is wise to avoid them. The map shows where you have already been.}
\aventura{2}{The Rescue of Sir Ragnar}
Sir Ragnar, one of the Emperor's mightiest knights, has been abducted. Ulag, the orc warlord, holds him prisoner.
\objetivo{Find Sir Ragnar and bring him safely to the stairs. Prince Magnus pays 200 gold coins to his rescuer. The reward may be shared, but nothing is paid if Sir Ragnar dies during the escape.}
\consejos{Finding the prisoner spreads the alarm through the stronghold: the whole map is revealed and the monsters know where you are. Sir Ragnar joins the party as another character, but he is weak, moves slowly and cannot fight: escort him. Search cleared rooms for treasure, checking for traps first if you see a chest.}
\aventura{3}{Lair of the Orc Warlord}
Prince Magnus has ordered Ulag hunted down for kidnapping Sir Ragnar.
\objetivo{Kill Ulag. Whoever succeeds receives 100 gold coins, and may keep all treasure found in his stronghold.}
\consejos{Ulag is not alone and hits harder than an ordinary orc. Attack together and reserve damage spells for him. Several treasures are hidden here, including a weapon, and at least one chest is trapped.}
\aventura{4}{Prince Magnus' Gold}
Three chests were stolen on their way to the Emperor. The thieves are an orc band hiding in the Black Mountains under Gulthor, a Chaos warrior.
\objetivo{Recover the chests with \emph{all} their gold and carry them out of the dungeon. The reward is 200 gold coins.}
\consejos{A hero carrying a chest has \pant{ROYAL GOLD} in their inventory and rolls only one movement die. Clear the return route first, and have unburdened heroes protect the carriers.}
\aventura{5}{Melar's Maze}
Long ago the mighty wizard Melar made a talisman that deepens its wearer's understanding of magic. Fearing it might fall into the hands of Morcar's allies, he hid it in his laboratory at the heart of a maze protected by traps and magical guardians. Spirits of those who died seeking it are said to haunt the place.
\objetivo{Find the Talisman of Lore and leave with it.}
\consejos{This adventure is full of traps and secret doors. Search each corridor section before advancing and let the dwarf lead. Not everything opens immediately; another discovery must come before the talisman. The talisman is ideal for the wizard or elf.}
\aventura{6}{Legacy of the Orc Warlord}
Grak, Ulag's offspring, has sworn revenge on his father's killers. After months of searching he ambushed you and imprisoned you while deciding your punishment. But the guard is asleep, and you have picked the cell lock with a rat's bone.
\objetivo{Recover your equipment and escape.}
\consejos{You start without weapons or armour, making every fight dangerous. Find the room where Grak keeps your possessions first. Until then, stay together and avoid monsters.}
\aventura{7}{The Stone Hunter}
Karlen, the Emperor's personal wizard, has disappeared. The Emperor fears he has been murdered or seduced by Chaos magic.
\objetivo{Discover what happened to Karlen and bring him to safety if alive. Each hero receives 100 gold coins on returning.}
\consejos{The title hints at what lurks here. Borin's Armour, one of the game's best items, and Karlen's chest are hidden in this dungeon. Search furnished rooms.}
\aventura{8}{The Fire Mage}
Orcs from the Black Mountains use fire magic in their raids. Balur, the Fire Mage, is believed to aid them with spells the Emperor's wizards cannot counter.
\objetivo{Enter Balur's lair beneath Black Fire Crag and destroy him. The Emperor rewards each hero.}
\consejos{No fire spell can harm Balur, so give the wizard other groups when sharing magic. The Wand of Recall is hidden in his lair.}
\aventura{9}{Race Against Time}
A guide has led you into a dungeon said to hold a great secret. After many dark corridors you reach a room with three doors. The guide puts out his torch and laughs as he bids farewell: it was a trap.
\objetivo{Escape alive from this forgotten hole.}
\consejos{You start without knowing where the exit is. Stay together and do not delay: monsters approach from every direction. There is gold for those who stop to search, but every turn counts.}
\aventura{10}{The Mysterious Castle}
Long ago the mad wizard Ollar discovered a gold mine entrance and protected it with a magical castle, full of enchanted portals and guarded by monsters trapped in time.
\objetivo{Find the mine entrance. Others tried, but the castle fooled them all.}
\consejos{Doors do not lead where you expect: crossing one sends heroes into a random room, easily scattering the party. Whoever finds the gold must decide whether to carry it. Its weight prevents attacking or defending, though it can always be dropped from the inventory.}
\aventura{11}{Bastion of Chaos}
The eastern lands are being plundered by orcs and goblins sheltering in an underground stronghold, the Bastion of Chaos, commanded by a small band of Chaos warriors.
\objetivo{Fight through and kill every monster. Rewards are 10 coins per goblin, 20 per orc, and 30 per fimir or Chaos warrior.}
\consejos{This adventure has the most fighting. Bring every hero and the best equipment you can afford. Advance room by room, clearing each before opening another door. One gargoyle looks like a statue, and will remain so while nobody touches what it guards.}
\aventura{12}{Barak Tor}
War with the eastern orcs approaches, and the Emperor must unite the lesser kingdoms. He needs the Star of the West, worn by legendary kings and Rogar himself. The gem lies in Barak Tor, the burial mound where the Witch Lord rests.
\objetivo{Find the Star of the West and leave with it. Delivering it earns 200 gold coins.}
\consejos{Barak Tor is a place of undead: skeletons, zombies and mummies, slow but hard to bring down. Beware of what you wake: some seals are better left unbroken, and cave-ins may cut off retreat.}
\aventura{13}{The Spirit Blade}
You have awakened the Witch Lord, King of the Dead and a powerful servant of Morcar. He must be destroyed before sending his undead army against the Emperor. Only one weapon can wound him: the Spirit Blade, forged by the dwarfs of the World's Edge Mountains and tempered in the elven fountain of Lebin. It lies in an ancient ruined temple.
\objetivo{Find the Spirit Blade.}
\consejos{The temple is well guarded, and the sword is not its only treasure. Give it to a good fighter: it rolls four dice against undead, and the next adventure cannot be won without it.}
\aventura{14}{Return to Barak Tor}
With the Spirit Blade in your possession you must return to Barak Tor. The Emperor has marched to meet the eastern orcs at Black Fire Pass. If you fail, the Witch Lord's undead will strike his army from behind and nothing will stop Chaos invading the land.
\objetivo{Defeat the Witch Lord.}
\consejos{The Witch Lord is immune to spells and ordinary weapons. Only the Spirit Blade's bearer can wound him. Everyone else must protect that hero and hold off the undead escort. Save healing spells for the bearer.}

\chapter{Return of the Witch Lord}
The adventure list's second page contains the expansion's ten adventures, continuing the story after \textit{Return to Barak Tor}. They are longer and harder than the earlier quests. Playing in order with well-equipped heroes is even more important.
\pantalla[0.7]{pergamino_exp_en}{The briefing for the expansion's first adventure.}
\subsection*{A message from Mentor}
``Worthy companions, we must act quickly: the Witch Lord is not dead!'' He escaped to Kalos, the fallen city. Morcar destroyed that beautiful city long ago and gave it to the Witch Lord. Beneath its ruins he built a mighty fortress, for his creatures cannot bear daylight. Once his strength returns, he will gather his undead army and attack the Empire. His warriors know no fear and fight until destroyed.

Many days across the scorched Plains of Death have brought you to Kalos. Throughout the journey you have felt a disturbing presence: from his high throne, the Witch Lord sees everyone approaching. Unless stopped soon, he will be joined by Skulmar, captain of the Forgotten Legion, and Kessandria, the Witch Queen. It is said that if both sit beside him, the bones of every fallen warrior will rise beneath the Black Banner.
\subsection*{What is new in the expansion}
\begin{itemize}
\item Heroes do not always start at the stairs. They often enter through a door at the dungeon's edge, and the exit may be another door they must find.
\item Between adventures heroes recover all body and mind points.
\item There are much stronger named enemies, new traps, and rooms with magical behaviour.
\end{itemize}
\dospantallas{sala_q10}{sala_q13}{The heroes, ready to descend once more.}
\aventura{1}{The Gate of Doom}
The fortress entrance is easy to find: nobody has bothered to hide it. Its outer door stands open, and many creatures have clearly passed through in recent days.
\objetivo{Make your way through the tunnels to the Gate of Doom.}
\consejos{The enemy has prepared a welcome: false doors will not open, and ambushers wait in rooms to emerge together. Search rooms: gold and more await those who look.}
\aventura{2}{The Cold Halls}
These icy passages are the resting place of the Spirit Riders, who once led the Witch Lord's skeleton cavalry into battle. They now dream of past glory, waiting for the Black Banner to call again.
\objetivo{Find the spiral stairs to continue.}
\consejos{Spirit Riders look like skeletons but attack and defend much better. A rotating room makes it impossible to predict which door you will leave by. A chest is protected by a poisoned needle: search for traps before opening it. Not every tomb should be disturbed.}
\aventura{3}{The Silent Passages}
These passages have been silent for over a thousand years. The last voices heard were King Agrain and his people rushing to defend the city. Ancient, terrible magic is said to work here.
\objetivo{Find the exit door.}
\consejos{A deadly mist, the breath of Chaos, moves through the corridors and harms anyone who is not evil. Stay out of its path. Rooms contain a trapped chest and some magical throwing weapons.}
\aventura{4}{The Halls of Vision}
King Agrain was a great king before Chaos, a feared warrior and a just lawgiver. He also experimented with magic, creating the Halls of Vision, a maze of magical rooms whose secret lies in the Keys of Agrain.
\objetivo{Find the Keys of Agrain and reach the exit door.}
\consejos{Four magical rooms, each guarded by a mummy, protect the king's secret laboratory. When a mummy falls, the doors change. The keys are in the laboratory, and are valuable for more than one reason.}
\aventura{5}{The Gate of Bellthor}
Here begins the Witch Lord's true domain: the underground fortress from which he plots the Empire's destruction. Loretome tells of a terrible guardian, Bellthor, watching the gate.
\objetivo{Face Bellthor. Only together can you defeat him.}
\consejos{Bellthor is a gargoyle whose poisonous breath attacks the mind. He will not move until all heroes stand before him. Enter together with as many body points as possible, and collect the chest gold first. Victory will have an unexpected price.}
\aventura{6}{The Halls of the Dead}
The wizard and dwarf wake in a cell, still dazed by poisonous gas. You are prisoners of the Witch Lord, who gathers his foul army here; the elf and barbarian have been taken to a secret place.
\objetivo{Rescue your companions and escape by the spiral stairs.}
\consejos{Only the wizard and dwarf play initially. They keep weapons, items and spells, but their gold is gone. The barbarian and elf are chained and unequipped: after freeing them, find the chest holding their possessions.}
\aventura{7}{The Forgotten Legion}
You have escaped the Witch Lord, but he will soon pursue you. These halls house the Forgotten Legion, his elite troops under Skulmar: warriors bearing the scars of a thousand battles, each a victory for Chaos.
\objetivo{Find the exit door.}
\consejos{The Legion waits motionless in magical ice that no weapon can break\ldots{} until the wrong door opens. Skulmar is stronger than any Chaos warrior and will try to flee if the fight turns against him. If killed, he leaves worthwhile spoils.}
\aventura{8}{The Forbidden City}
No man has entered the Forbidden City, and even Loretome knows little of what awaits. Its passages are said to be full of undead, Morcar's mindless servants, condemned to wander forever.
\objetivo{Cross the city and find the exit.}
\consejos{The undead never run out: more appear every turn, so staying to fight is pointless. The city is collapsing too. Treasure rewards those who risk searching, but some is poisoned.}
\aventura{9}{The Last Gate}
This is the last gate before the Witch Lord's court. The passages beyond are guarded by the Doomguard, Chaos warriors mightier than any you have fought, and hold Kessandria's chambers, the Witch Queen.
\objetivo{Find the spiral stairs leading to the Witch Lord.}
\consejos{The Doomguard attack with four dice and defend with six. Kessandria commands Chaos magic and fears only fire spells: save them for her.}
\aventura{10}{The Court of the Witch Lord}
The Witch Lord has watched your advance. Repeatedly he has tried to destroy you, and repeatedly you have thwarted him. Now he has fled to his throne room.
\objetivo{Fight the final battle there. This time he must not escape.}
\consejos{The Witch Lord is stronger than at Barak Tor, knows Chaos spells and is escorted by Doomguard warriors. Only the Spirit Blade, Fire of Wrath, Ball of Flame and magical throwing daggers can hurt him. Do not attack the orc statues blocking the corridors: they take no damage and weapons break against them.}
\subsection*{Conclusion}
If the Witch Lord falls, his undead army will remain asleep beneath the earth and the magic controlling it will weaken. But Mentor warns that victory is no excuse to rest. The servants who escaped will seek revenge, and Skulmar, believed dead, has fled Kalos with many of his master's spell books. For now you have earned a respite: the Emperor awaits you in the Great Palace.
