HeroQuest MSX2 - Version 1.0
An unofficial homage port by DiHalt Studio, 2026.

Requirements: MSX2 or later, 64 KB RAM, 128 KB VRAM.
Cartridge: ASCII16 MegaROM, 2 MiB. Optional MoonSound/OPL4 audio.

heroquest.rom is the normal game. Select ASCII16 explicitly in an emulator
or use an ASCII16-compatible flash cartridge with space for a 2 MiB image.

openmsx -machine Philips_NMS_8250 -carta heroquest.rom -romtype ASCII16
Add -ext moonsound for OPL4 music. The internal PSG works without it.

After the opening picture, choose ENGLISH or ESPANOL with up/down and
confirm with SPACE, RETURN or joystick fire. The intro and all 24
adventures use the selected language until restart.

manual-en.pdf: English user manual, with English screenshots.
manual-es.pdf: Manual en espanol, con capturas en espanol.
extras/heroquest-cheat.rom: optional cheat edition, heroes win combat
and take no damage. Use heroquest.rom for normal play.

Source, build instructions and manuals:
https://github.com/DiHaltStudio/heroquest-msx2

Original game: Gremlin Graphics, 1991. Based on the HeroQuest board game.
